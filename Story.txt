The night was silent… until a strange noise shattered the darkness.

You wake suddenly, your heart pounding.
Something moved outside your room.

Half asleep, you follow the sound through the cold night air.
Then you see it.

A small goblin, clutching something in its hands, fleeing into the shadows.

“Hey! Stop!”

You chase after it instinctively… but after only a few steps, you freeze.

What… did it steal?

You try to remember, but your mind feels empty.
Not just the stolen object — everything.

Your name...
Your past...
Why you are here.

Gone.

Only fragments remain deep within your mind.

Now, the only path forward is through the creatures standing in your way.

You take a moment and try to remember.
